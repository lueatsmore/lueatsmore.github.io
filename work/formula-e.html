Overview

During my time as Marketing Manager at TCS, I led the local marketing activation around the Shanghai E-Prix for three consecutive years, turning TCS' global sports sponsorship asset as the title sponsor and technology partner of Jaguar TCS Racing into a platform for brand visibility, client engagement, and employee participation in the local market.

Rather than treating Formula E as a race-day branding opportunity, I built the Shanghai E-Prix into a broader business engagement platform for TCS China. The program connected sustainability, technology innovation, customer experience, senior stakeholder engagement, and employee participation, turning a global sponsorship asset into a locally relevant marketing engine.

My Role

I was responsible for the overall activity planning and local execution, including client hospitality, business sessions, media and content communications, digital campaigns, employee engagement, on-site experience design, and cross-functional coordination.

The project required close collaboration with local business teams, the global sports sponsorship team, Global Communications and Social Media teams, the India-based content support team, race and team partners, media partners, influencers, agencies, and suppliers. My role was to ensure that the activation stayed aligned with TCS' global sponsorship narrative while also addressing the relationship-building and communications needs of the local market.

The Challenge

TCS' partnership with Jaguar TCS Racing carries strong global brand value. In China, the key challenge was how to move this asset beyond brand visibility and turn it into an experience that people could participate in, share, and build relationships around.

Within a limited race-week window, I needed to serve multiple goals at once: strengthen connections with high-value clients, reinforce TCS' association with technology and sustainability, create media and social content opportunities, and enable employees to participate in this global sponsorship platform.

The Approach

I positioned the Shanghai E-Prix as a multi-touchpoint annual marketing platform, rather than a standalone race hospitality event.

1
Client Engagement
Designed differentiated hospitality experiences including senior client invitations, family-friendly race viewing, driver interaction opportunities, and on-site circuit experiences. These moments created a relaxed but high-quality environment for clients to build deeper connections with the TCS team.
2
Thought Leadership
Planned business sessions around AI, sports technology, sustainability, and business innovation — extending the momentum of the race into deeper conversations with clients and business partners.
3
Communications
Drove digital and offline touchpoints including a Show Car flash store, H5 interactive experience, and social media advertising. Worked closely with the Communications team to activate media and influencer programs. By connecting the race experience, team assets, and TCS' technology narrative, these initiatives helped the local market understand TCS' role not only as a sponsor, but as a technology enabler.
4
Employee Engagement
Created internal programs linked to sustainability, encouraging employees to participate through low-carbon commuting, green lifestyle actions, and interactive challenges. As AI became a key company theme, the employee program evolved into an AI Creative Challenge — connecting race engagement with creative AI expression and expanding participation from "watching the race" to "co-creating content."
"Effective sponsorship marketing is not about placing the logo in a more visible position. It is about finding the connection between brand, business, clients, and local culture — then turning that connection into market actions that people can experience, discuss, and remember."
The Impact
Reputation Amplified TCS China's local brand presence through an integrated reputation engine combining media and creator programs, Show Car showcase, and digital campaigns that extended the sponsorship story beyond race day.
Revenue Supported business development by turning race-week engagement into senior client conversations, business sessions, and relationship-building moments with high-value accounts.
Relationship Deepened stakeholder relationships across clients, media, employees, and global teams by creating meaningful ways for each audience to participate in the Shanghai E-Prix platform.

After three years of continuous iteration, the project evolved from an annual sponsorship activation into one of TCS China's signature integrated marketing programs. Client participation continued to grow, reaching a new high in the final year, while media, digital engagement, and employee participation became part of a more complete activation loop.

More importantly, this project helped me rethink sports sponsorship as an operable marketing asset rather than a brand exposure opportunity. It can support client relationships, business conversations, brand storytelling, employee engagement, and market communications, while becoming stronger through year-by-year learning and optimisation.

What I Learned

This project deepened my understanding of sponsorship activation in B2B marketing. Effective sponsorship marketing is not about placing the logo in a more visible position. It is about finding the connection between brand, business, clients, and local culture, then turning that connection into market actions that people can experience, discuss, and remember.
